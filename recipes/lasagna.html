Lasagna

Description

Sheets of pasta layered with a meaty, saucy filling

Ingredients

  • Pasta sheets
  • Tomatoes
  • Ricotta cheese
  • olive oil
  • minced lamb
  • garlic
  • basil
  • salt
  • black pepper

Steps

  1. Prepare filling by cooking tomatoes, cheese, lamb, garlic and basil in olive oil with salt and pepper.
  2. Layer the filling with the pasta, brush it in oil then microwave it to 180 degrees in Celcius.
  3. Let it cool, garnish with cheese and basil and finally bon appetit!
